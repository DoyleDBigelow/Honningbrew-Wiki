## Hit Points [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=1)

Hit Dice: 1d8 per bard level

Hit Points at 1st Level: 8 + your Constitution modifier

Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per bard level after 1st

## Starting Proficiencies [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=2)

You are proficient with the following items, in addition to any proficiencies provided by your race or background.

Armor: Light Armor

Weapons: daggers

Tools: three musical instruments of your choice

Skills: Choose any three.

  

## Starting Equipment [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=3)

You start with the following items, plus anything provided by your background.

• an iron dagger

• (a) a Diplomat's Pack or (b) an Entertainer's Pack

• a lute, drum, and flute.

  

## Bardic Inspiration [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=4)

You can inspire others through stirring words or music. To do so, you use a Bonus Action on your turn to choose one creature other than yourself within 60 feet of you who can hear you. That creature gains one Bardic Inspiration die, a d6.

Once within the next 10 minutes, the creature can roll the die and add the number rolled to one ability check, Attack roll, or saving throw it makes. The creature can wait until after it rolls the d20 before deciding to use the Bardic Inspiration die, but must decide before the DM says whether the roll succeeds or fails. Once the Bardic Inspiration die is rolled, it is lost. A creature can have only one Bardic Inspiration die at a time.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (a minimum of once). You regain any expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Your Bardic Inspiration die changes when you reach certain levels in this class. The die becomes a d8 at 5th level, a d10 at 10th level, and a d12 at 15th level.

## Songs [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=5)

Bards do not have spells, instead they have the ability to play songs. Songs are like spells, but they are “cast” through the use of your musical instruments. Any spell on the spell list highlighted in green can be selected as a bard song. You ignore the listed range for the spell. The range for your songs is simply all creatures within earshot.

Minor Songs (Cantrips)

You know two Minor Songs of your choice from the bard song list. You learn additional Minor Songs of your choice at higher levels, learning a 3rd minor song at 4th level and a 4th at 10th level.

Powerful Song Slots (Leveled Songs)

The Bard table shows how many Song Slots you have to play your songs of 1st level and higher. To play one of these songs, you must expend a slot of the song’s level or higher. You regain all expended Spell Slots when you finish a Long Rest.

Songs Known of 1st Level and Higher

You know four 1st-level Songs of your choice from the bard song list.

You learn an additional bard song of your choice at each level except 12th, 16th, 19th, and 20th. Each of these songs must be of a level for which you have Song Slots. For instance, when you reach 3rd level in this class, you can learn one new song of 1st or 2nd level.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the bard Songs you know and replace it with another Song from the bard song list, which also must be of a level for which you have Song Slots.

Musical Ability

Charisma is your “spellcasting” ability for your bard songs. Your magic comes from the heart and soul you pour into the performance of your music or oration. You use your Charisma whenever a song refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Charisma modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a bard song you play and when making an Attack roll with one.

Spell save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier

Spell Attack modifier = your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier

## Jack of All Trades [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=6)

Starting at 2nd level, you can add half your proficiency bonus, rounded down, to any ability check you make that doesn't already include your proficiency bonus.

  

## Song of Rest [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=7)

Beginning at 2nd level, you can use soothing music or oration to help revitalize your wounded allies during a Short Rest. If you or any friendly creatures who can hear your performance regain hit points by spending Hit Dice at the end of the Short Rest, each of those creatures regains an extra 1d6 hit points.

The extra hit points increase when you reach certain levels in this class: to 1d8 at 9th level, to 1d10 at 13th level, and to 1d12 at 17th level.

## Bard College [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=8)

At 3rd level, you delve into the advanced techniques of a bard college of your choice, such as the College of Lore. Your choice grants you features at 3rd level and again at 6th and 14th level.

## Expertise [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=9)

At 3rd level, choose two of your skill proficiencies. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either of the chosen proficiencies.

At 10th level, you can choose another two skill proficiencies to gain this benefit.

## Ability Score Improvement [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=10)

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two Ability Scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can’t increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

## Font of Inspiration [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=11)

Beginning when you reach 5th level, you regain all of your expended uses of Bardic Inspiration when you finish a short or Long Rest.

## Countercharm [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=12)

At 6th level, you gain the ability to use musical notes or words of power to disrupt mind-influencing effects. As an action, you can start a performance that lasts until the end of your next turn. During that time, you and any friendly creatures within 30 feet of you have advantage on saving throws against being Frightened or Charmed. A creature must be able to hear you to gain this benefit. The performance ends early if you are Incapacitated or silenced or if you voluntarily end it (no action required).

## Magical Secrets [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=13)

By 10th level, you have plundered cultural knowledge from a wide spectrum of disciplines. Choose two Ancient Songs. The chosen songs count as bard songs for you and are included in the number in the Songs Known column of the Bard table.

You learn two additional ancient songs at 14th level and again at 18th level.

## Superior Inspiration [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=14)

At 20th level, when you roll initiative and have no uses of Bardic Inspiration left, you regain one use.

## College of Lore [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=15)

Bards of the College of Lore know something about most things, collecting bits of knowledge from sources as diverse as scholarly tomes and peasant tales. Whether singing folk ballads in taverns or elaborate compositions in royal courts, these bards use their gifts to hold audiences spellbound. When the applause dies down, the audience members might find themselves questioning everything they held to be true, from their faith in the priesthood of the local temple to their loyalty to the king.

The loyalty of these bards lies in the pursuit of beauty and truth, not in fealty to a monarch or following the tenets of a deity. A noble who keeps such a bard as a herald or advisor knows that the bard would rather be honest than politic.

The college’s members gather in libraries and sometimes in actual colleges, complete with classrooms and dormitories, to share their lore with one another. They also meet at festivals or affairs of state, where they can expose corruption, unravel lies, and poke fun at self-important figures of authority.

### Bonus Proficiencies [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=16)

When you join the College of Lore at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with three skills of your choice.

### Cutting Words [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=17)

Also at 3rd level, you learn how to use your wit to distract, confuse, and otherwise sap the confidence and competence of others. When a creature that you can see within 60 feet of you makes an Attack roll, an ability check, or a damage roll, you can use your reaction to expend one of your uses of Bardic Inspiration, rolling a Bardic Inspiration die and subtracting the number rolled from the creature’s roll. You can choose to use this feature after the creature makes its roll, but before the GM determines whether the Attack roll or ability check succeeds or fails, or before the creature deals its damage. The creature is immune if it can’t hear you or if it’s immune to being Charmed.

### Songs of the Ancient [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=18)

At 6th level, you learn two ancient songs of your choice.The chosen songs count as bard Songs for you but don’t count against the number of bard Songs you know.

### Peerless Skill [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=19)

Starting at 14th level, when you make an ability check, you can expend one use of Bardic Inspiration. Roll a Bardic Inspiration die and add the number rolled to your ability check. You can choose to do so after you roll the die for the ability check, but before the GM tells you whether you succeed or fail.

## College of Valor [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=20)

Bards of the College of Valor are daring skalds whose tales keep alive the memory of the great heroes of the past, and thereby inspire a new generation of heroes. These bards gather in mead halls or around great bonfires to sing the deeds of the mighty, both past and present. They travel the land to witness great events firsthand and to ensure that the memory of those events doesn't pass from the world. With their songs, they inspire others to reach the same heights of accomplishment as the heroes of old.

### Bonus Proficiencies [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=21)

When you join the College of Valor at 3rd level, you gain proficiency with medium armor, shields, and martial weapons.

### Combat Inspiration [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=22)

Also at 3rd level, you learn to inspire others in battle. A creature that has a Bardic Inspiration die from you can roll that die and add the number rolled to a weapon damage roll it just made. Alternatively, when an attack roll is made against the creature, it can use its reaction to roll the Bardic Inspiration die and add the number rolled to its AC against that attack, after seeing the roll but before knowing whether it hits or misses.

### Extra Attack [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=23)

Starting at 6th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### Battle Song [Edit](http://engl393-dnd5th.wikia.com/wiki/Bard?action=edit&section=24)

At 14th level, you have mastered the art of weaving music and weapon use into a single harmonious act. When you use your action to play a song, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.
