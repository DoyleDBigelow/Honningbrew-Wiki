Lore Description: The alchemist uses his knowledge of nature to create powerful potions and deadly poisons.
Dev Description: A potion slinging class, capable of harm as much as they are hurt. Rather then spell slots you will Home Alone style prep your bag for situations that may be fall you. You will make money with this class. This class prefers the back line.
Hit Die: d8
Primary Attributes: Dexterity, Wisdom
Saving Throws: Dexterity, Wisdom
Armor Proficiencies: Light Armor
Weapon Proficiencies: Simple Weapons, Sling
Tool Proficiencies: Brewer's Supplies
Skills: Choose 3: Acrobatics, Arcana, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, and Survival

For convenience: When using a sling to throw bottles at targets. You have a range of 30/120. 30ft is your effective range, where 120ft is the long range. When firing in the long range you have disadvantage on attack rolls. Bottles do 1d4 + Dexterity Mod damage when they break, then the potion splashed on the target seeps into the wounds. The roll to hit with a bottle is: d20 + Dexterity Mod + Proficiency Bonus (if you are proficient with the weapon)
Your daily concoctions brew to a max limit every day, see table. You can obtain more potions by simply owning fewer potions. It's easiest to give them to party members, or even sell them.

**Level 1:**
*Bottle Cantrips* - You can use the cantrip [[Alchemist's Best Friend]] to sculpt bottles, phials, and other glassware from sand or dirt. When inspected, the glassware is of moderate quality.
*Morning Brew* - You are able to prepare a daily number of concoctions from your known recipes during a long rest. It costs nothing to brew them, but you will need bottles. All other potion production takes 30 minutes for a single potion, and requires access to heat and water.

**Level 2:**
*Bottle Marksmen* - When you declare an attack with a bottle, (with or without potion) you may take a -2 penalty to hit the opponent with the bottle. If you succeed despite this penalty, the glass breaks into an enemies eyes causing them to have disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn. The bottle does an additional 1d4 damage. If this is the third time youve done this to this enemy they suffer blindness until the glass is extracted.

**Sub-Class:**
You are offered two subclasses each with a distinct effect on your future potential:

*Dawn Alchemist* - You exist to heal the world, sure you might have other hobbies but having solutions to people's problems brings you great joy as an alchemist. At level 2 when you pick this option your restorative potions have an improved recipe, and can be used twice before being depleted (unless thrown).

*Dusk Alchemist* - You become more proficient with poisons and stealth. You prefer to watch victims fall ill to your poison unwittingly. It has always yielded better research. At level 2 when you pick this option gain one skill proficiency in either Stealth, or Sleight of Hand. You may affect targets with your poisons by placing them in their pockets. Additionally, poisons you produce deal 1d6 necrotic damage, in addition to effects and damage they deal.

**Level 3:**
*Daycare* - You are naturally gifted at protecting your potions. If someone attempts to steal a potion from you for any reason they have disadvantage on skill checks to do so. Additionally, you are able to adjust your body and bag quickly from years of instinct. Whether this is from a trip, a trap, a bashing, or bludgeoning you can typically keep your potions safe from danger.

*Everfull Bag* - You always have the ingredients on hand to procure your potions as long as you have your brewer's supplies. You can spend 10 minutes during your adventure to produce a batch of potions from your recipes. When you make potions during your daily concoction, all potions you know the recipe for count against how many you many brew.

**Level 4:**
ASI- You can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. You cannot increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

**Level 5:**
*Soulbond Alchemy* - When you create your daily concoctions, you may choose to forge a soulbond with a single potion daily allowing an additional effect . Each subclass has different effects.
Dawn Sub-class: When a healing potion is consumed that you are soulbound to, it heals you for what it heals your target. Half that much becomes temporary HP if you are at full health.
Dusk Sub-class: When a poison you are soulbound to deals damage to a target, you may become invisible for 30 seconds.
