A Points-based Magicka system
Magicka Pool is calculated:
2+(PlayerLevel * Max Magicka per Spell)

| Level | Magicka Pool | Max Level of Spell | Max Magicka per Spell | Calculated |

*- You may in traditional D&D cast spells at higher levels a limited number of times per day. In this magicka system, 6th level spells and higher are considered "Major Arcana" while you may cast as many 2nd levels spells as you want like they are 9th level, only a real 9th level spell counts against your Major Arcana casts for the day. Casters may only cast as many Major Arcana per day as half their level rounded down.
