Description: You used to be a fletcher. Why is this not Tradesmen? Well you can make arrows so that's something different.

Additional Effects:
- Add one Skill Proficiency of your choice.
- You can craft arrows, provided you have the required resources. You do it quickly, with adept hands. Even basic arrows of wood and tree leaves.
- You can produce a moderate quality wooden bow, given you have the resources required to do so.

Starting Equipment:
* a backpack in design of your choice
* a bedroll
* a mess kit
* a tinderbox
* 10 torches
* 10 days of rations
* a waterskin
* 75 feet of hempen rope
* a bottle of lacquer
* a roll of wrap for bow grips (8 bows)
