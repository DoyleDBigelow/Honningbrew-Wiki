Description: Workshop your origin story with your DM. Essentially, picking this background enabled you to be cursed from the start of the adventure. This is highly useful for flavor reasons, but is also highly customizable! For every positive, there is a negative however.
Additional Effects:
- Add one Skill Proficiency of your choice.
- Add a curse from a Daedra to your character sheet. This will afflict you until the parameters set by your DM have met.
- You can place your hand on daedric writing and gleam a poor understanding of it's meaning.

Starting Equipment:
* a backpack in design of your choice
* a bedroll
* a mess kit
* a tinderbox
* 10 torches
* 10 days of rations
* a waterskin
* 50 feet of hempen rope
