### Fighter
A Fighter is a master in close quarters combat, focusing on using their fists and legs as instruments of destruction, turning their enemies into bloody masses.

### Class Features

As a Fighter you gain the following class features.

Hit Points

Hit Dice: 1d8 per Bone Breaker level

Hit Points at 1st Level: 10 + Constitution modifier

Hit Points at Higher Levels: 1d8 + Constitution modifier per Bone Breaker level after 1st

Proficiencies

Armor: none

Weapons: simple weapons and unarmed attacks

Tools: none

Saving Throws: strength or dexterity, constitution

Skills: choose two from acrobatics, athletics, medicine, investigation, sleight of hand or intimidation

Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- Any simple weapon
    
- (a) Dungeoneers pack or (b) Explorers pack
    
- common clothes

### Unarmored Defense

At first level you have gained a skilled ability to block and avoid damage. When not wearing armor, your AC = 10 + (Strength or Dexterity) + Constitution Modifier

### Martial Arts

At first level you have a powerful ability to fight with your fists and feet, when not holding a weapon you can deal damage with your fists and legs, this damage scales with your level appropriately, as shown on the class table. When wielding a simple weapon you can make an unarmed strike with the bonus action. You can use ether dexterity or strength as a modifier for your unarmed strikes

### Skilled Strikes

At first level, you have spent years training and have developed specialized fighting moves. When making an attack with an unarmed attack you can choose to make a leg or fist attack before you make the attack roll. These moves upgrade at 6th level, 14th level, and 18th level.

Kick Attack: You make a powerful kick attack, granting a +2 to damage, at 6th level to +4 damage, at the 14th level its +6 to damage, and at 18th level, it becomes +8 to damage, However, this gives you Disadvantage on your accuracy roll for this attack

Fist Attack: You make an accurate punch attack, granting you Advantage to your accuracy roll, but doing only half total damage (Rounded down to a minimum of 1) At level 6 the enemy makes a strength saving throw (DC 10 at level 1, then 13 on level 6, 16 on level 14 and 16 level 18) on a fail, he is knocked prone. At level 18 you can choose to stun him instead. The Stun lasts until the enemy next turn. You need to finish a long rest before you can use stun a creature with this feature again.

### Unarmored Movement

At 2nd level you've learned to move swifter in less time. When you aren't wearing armor you have increased speed, as shown on the class table. At 9th level you can move along vertical surfaces without falling during the move and at 15th level you can run across water without falling during the movement.

### Battle Stance

At 2nd level you gain access to ancient stances to alter the tide of the battle. The list of stances you have access to grows as you level up but at 2nd level you only have access to the Stance Of Wind, Stance Of The Bear and Stance Of The Cobra. It requires the use of an action to activate a stance, and can only be used without a weapon in hand. Once a stance has been activated you cannot move, for if you do the stance ends. The effect of a stance can only be used once per activation.

Stance Of The Bear: when used during the next melee attack action each hit deals an extra amount of damage equal to double your proficiency bonus.

Stance Of The Wind: when used the next enemy melee attacks against you have disadvantage.

Stance Of The Cobra: when used the next melee attack action you make has advantage.

### Striker Archetype

At 3rd level you chose a Striker Archetype, the Kickboxer or the Pugilist.

### Stance Of Deflection

At 3rd you have finally developed a technique to deal with those pesky ranged attacks. You have gained access to the Stance Of Deflection.

Stance Of Deflection: When used the next ranged attack that hits you you can catch, roll 1d10 + dexterity or strength + Bone breaker level. If you catch a ranged attack you can then uses your reaction to throw it at someone, this uses your unarmed strike damage die or the weapons initial damage die, whichever you choose.

#### Ability Score Increase

When you reach 4th level, and again at 6th, 8th, 12th, 16th and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of you choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

### Second Wind

At 4th level you can draw energy from deep inside, using an action you can heal one hit die + Your Bone Breaker level while in combat. You can only do this once per long rest. You spend one of your normal 3 hit dies by using this action.

### Extra Attack

At 5th level you can now attack twice instead of once. This upgrades at 14th level, so you can attack three times.

### Stunning Stance

At 5th level your power and skill pulses through your body, this gives access to the Stunning Stance.

Stunning Stance: When used the next attack you make against a creature, the creature must make a constitution saving throw, DC = 10 + proficiency bonus + dexterity or strength, and on a failure the creature is stunned until the end of your next turn.

### Evasion

At 7th level your skills and speed can be brought to levels of near-superhuman levels. You can now use your reaction when hit with a area of effect attack that causes you to make a dexterity check, so that if you fail the check you take half damage and if you succeed you take no damage.

### Fear No Man

At 7th level you can use an action to end one effect on you that is causing you to be charmed or frightened.

### Stance Upgrade

At 10th level your stance's have increased in power and speed. You now need to only take a bonus action to activate a stance.

### Stance Of Redirection

At 13th level you have trained to gain the ability to redirect attacks. You have gained access to the Stance Of Redirection

Stance Of Redirection: When used, the next melee attack used against you automatically hits and you can use the enemy's momentum against them and lessen the damage from the attack, by rolling 1d12 + dexterity or strength modifier + Bone Breaker level, if you lessen the damage to under 0 you can throw them a distance equal to the amount of the space in between your roll and the damage of the attack. If the creature hits an object that isn't the floor the creature takes 1d6 per 5ft thrown, e,g if a creature rolls a 20 for damage and you roll a 7 on the d12 and you level is 13 and your dexterity modifier is +5, that totals to 25, so you can throw the creature 5ft and if the creature hits a wall during the throw it would take 1d6 damage. You can only use this on a creature that is your size or smaller.

### Hell Hound's Hunger

At 20th level your power has become overwhelming. You can now increase two of the following stats; Strength, Dexterity and Constitution by four, or one of them by 8, your new maximum for these stats are 24, or 28 if you increase it by 8.

### Striker Archetypes

## Pugilist

The Pugilist is a fighter that focuses on strength and pure defense. The DC for your attacks that require a saving throw = 8 + proficiency bonus + strength bonus.

### Brawler’s Stamina

At 3rd level you have been training to become stronger and be able to last longer. You gain proficiency in athletics, and if you already are, you double your proficiency bonus for this skill.

### Battle Block

At 6th level you have the ability to block. Blocking requires an action to do, meaning that you gain an AC bonus of +4. Whilst blocking, if you move your AC bonus from blocking goes down to +2.

### Iron Block

At 11th level you have increased your ability to block to the extreme. The AC bonus you get is now increase to +6 whilst still and +3 whilst moving. Blocking now only takes a bonus action.

### Stance Of The Dragon

At 17th level you have the strength and rage brimming within you. You gain access to the Stance Of The Dragon.

Stance Of The Dragon: when used, for the next minute, you gain the ability to (for 2 rounds) do 4D20 dmg with your Fist attack, and you gain a bonus to AC equals to your strength modifier + your proficiency bonus. Also when this is active your Fist attacks don't have a penalty to damage rolls. When this effect ends, you take 1 points of exhaustion.

## Kickboxer

The Kickboxer is dexterous warrior that focuses on kicks and deals high damage. The DC for your attacks that require a saving throw = 8 + proficiency bonus + dexterity bonus.

### Acrobatic Battler

At 3rd level your speed and acrobatics have made you become faster and incredibly agile. You gain proficiency in acrobatics, and if you already are you double your proficiency bonus.

### Drop Kick

At 6th level you have learned how to use your momentum to hit enemies harder than ever. You gain access to the Drop Kick ability. Using this takes your entire action to do.

Drop Kick: when you use this attack you jump into the air and propel your legs forwards like a cannon, causing your target to make a dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + dexterity modifier + proficiency bonus) and on a failed save they take full damage and ether get knocked prone or be pushed back 20ft, your choice, on a successful save they take half damage and don't suffer either effect. Using this ability causes you to make a dexterity saving throw (DC = 15) else be knocked prone upon landing.

### Bicycle Kick

At 11th level you have the ability to unleash a flurry of kicks. You gain access to the Bicycle Kick ability.

Bicycle Kick: You leap into the air and land a flurry of kicks. You do an extra amount of Kick attacks, without the disadvantage/subtraction to hit, equal to your proficiency modifier, on top of the amount you already have. Once you finish the attacks you must make a dexterity saving throw (DC = 18) or be knocked prone upon landing.

Note: It is customary, though not required, to unleash a wild scream upon initiating the Bicycle Kick.

### Stance Of The Hydra

At 17th level your power and speed have increased to that of which is not of this world. You gain access to the Stance Of The Hydra.

Stance Of The Hydra: when used for the next minute you can move twice your movement speed and your Kicks deal maximum damage, also when this is active your Kick attacks don't have a penalty to attack rolls, and the DC for the Drop Kick and Bicycle Kick lowers by 2. When you finish doing this you gain 1 point of exhaustion.
