*“A Breton knows that real power lies in the ink of a courtly contract, the sharp edge of a knight's blade, and the perfect counter-spell cast from the shadows of the throne room.”*
— *Lady Eloise, Countess of Wayrest*

Inhabiting the picturesque, fractured province of High Rock, the Bretons—or *Manmer*, as they are historically named—are a unique race of human-elven hybrids who perfectly bridge the gap between mortal ambition and arcane mastery. Centuries ago, elven overlords of the Direnni Clan interbred with their human subjects, creating a distinct population of half-elven bloodlines. While the Bretons appear predominantly human to a casual observer, their elven heritage burns brightly in their natural affinity for the arcane, making them the most universally gifted magic users, alchemists, and spell-weavers of all the human races on Tamriel. 

> [!info] The Feudal Landscape of High Rock
> High Rock is a beautiful yet notoriously fractured land of rolling green hills, soaring coastal cliffs, and heavily fortified stone castles. Politically, the Bretons operate under a highly complex, fiercely competitive feudal system. While the entire province will reluctantly unite under a single banner to defend against massive external threats, their internal reality is one of endless political theater. High Rock is carved into countless shifting baronies, duchies, and minor kingdoms ruled by scheming lords, ambitious merchant princes, and knightly orders who constantly wage shadow wars, economic blockades, and localized military campaigns for absolute power.

### Courtiers and Knights
This environment of endless social maneuvering has bred a culture of unparalleled ambition, wit, and adaptability. In Breton society, there is no greater calling than "doing better"—a cultural obsession where even the lowest-born peasant believes they can ascend to the nobility through clever diplomacy, military valor, or arcane brilliance. They value high culture, poetry, and courtly romance, balancing this refinement with a fierce knightly tradition. A Breton is just as likely to outmaneuver an adversary with a hidden poison or a forged trade treaty as they are to challenge them to an honorable duel with a beautifully balanced longsword.

### Names & Culture
Breton naming conventions mirror their aristocratic and courtly society. They always utilize both a given name and a family surname, heavily drawing inspiration from traditional real-world Old English, French, and Gaelic linguistic patterns.
* **Naming Conventions:** [UESP Breton Names Reference](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Breton_Names)

### Breton Traits
As a Breton, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Bretons are among the more compact human races in Tamriel, typically standing between 5’4" and 5’8" feet tall. While they possess a thoroughly human appearance at a distance, closer inspection reveals their elegant elven heritage through fine, aristocratic facial structures, slightly high cheekbones, and slightly pointed or tapered ears.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Tamrielic (Common) and one extra language of your choice.
