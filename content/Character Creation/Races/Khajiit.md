*“Do not mistake the velvet step for weakness, traveler. A cat may play with a ball of yarn, but those same paws can tear the throat from a dunmer slaver before he can clear his blade.”*
— *Ri'Sadd, Khajiit Merchant Lord*

Native to the arid wastes and dense, sweltering jungles of Elsweyr, the Khajiit are a fiercely independent, cat-like race known across Tamriel for their natural agility, sharp intellect, and fluid perception of ownership. While outsiders often stereotype them as mere thieves, skooma smugglers, or unpredictable rogues, the Khajiit possess a deeply spiritual, ancient culture governed by the cosmic dance of the moons. They are adaptable survivors, matching a feline grace with a sharp, calculating wit that makes them formidable warriors, peerless scouts, and—on rare occasions—eccentric wielders of arcane magic.

> [!info] The Lunar Lattice
> The most defining characteristic of Khajiiti biology is the *Ja-Kha'jay*, or the Lunar Lattice. A Khajiit’s physical form is not determined by their parentage, but entirely by the phases of Tamriel's twin moons, Jone and Jode, at the moment of their birth. A single litter can produce a massive, quadrapedal Senche-raht battle-cat and a diminutive, magic-wielding Alfiq that resembles a common housecat. Regardless of their shape, all Khajiit share a deep, spiritual reverence for the moons and their creator deity, Azurah—the Daedric Prince of Dusk and Dawn—who bound their souls to the silver tides of the cosmos.

### Pride and Prejudice
Because of their predatory heritage, Khajiit often eschew traditional steel weaponry, preferring the silent reliability of their own retractable claws. They look at the rigid legalities of the Empire with an amused detachment; to a Khajiit, if an item is left unguarded, the cosmos clearly intended for a more attentive soul to claim it. This fluid morality, combined with their dominance over the lucrative but illicit skooma trade, means Khajiit are frequently viewed with suspicion by other races. Consequently, they are rarely found at sea unless commanding a pirate vessel or running a smuggling operation, and they rarely establish permanent roots in foreign lands.

### Names & Culture
Khajiit names are deeply tied to their identity and social standing, utilizing sharp, honorific prefixes or suffixes (such as *Dar* for thief, *Do* for warrior, or *M/Ma* for child/apprentice) that evolve over the course of their lives. 
* **Naming Conventions:** [UESP Khajiit Names Reference](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Khajiit_Names)

### Khajiit Traits
As a Khajiit, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Ta’agra.

## Subrace: Cathay
While the Lunar Lattice creates over a dozen distinct breeds, the **Cathay** are by far the most common Khajiit seen wandering beyond the borders of Elsweyr. Born when Jone is full and Jode is waning, they represent the perfect balance of feline agility and humanoid versatility, making them excellent choices for wandering mercenaries, specialized thieves, and worldly scholars.

### Appearance & Demeanor
The Cathay are lithe, cat-like humanoids standing between 5’4" and 5’8" feet tall. Their bodies are completely covered in a coat of sleek, soft fur that ranges from sandy-tan and spotted leopard patterns to striking midnight black. They possess highly expressive feline faces, complete with slitted eyes, sensitive whiskers, and long, twitching tails used for perfect equilibrium. Cathay move with an effortless, liquid grace that makes even a casual stride look like a predatory stalk. They are highly social, possessing a purring, sibilant accent when speaking Common, and they tend to hide a razor-sharp intellect behind an air of casual, playful curiosity.

## Subrace: Cathay-Raht
Commonly referred to by outsiders as the "Jaguar-men," the **Cathay-Raht** are born when both Jone and Jode are full in the night sky. Larger, heavier, and far more imposing than their Cathay cousins, they are the vanguard and guardians of the Khajiiti kingdoms, born with a physical build meant to endure the frontline chaos of war.

### Appearance & Demeanor
Standing a formidable 5’8" to 6’0" feet tall with a thick, heavily muscled frame, the Cathay-Raht command immediate attention. Their fur coats are notably denser and frequently feature bold, jaguar-like rosettes or thick tiger striping. Their claws are longer and thicker than the standard Cathay, built less for delicate thievery and more for crushing armor. Despite their bulk, they retain the supernatural balance of their kin, though their movements carry a heavy, menacing power. While less inclined to playful curiosity, Cathay-Raht possess a fiercely protective nature, a stoic patience, and a thunderous growl that can unnerve even the bravest adversaries.
