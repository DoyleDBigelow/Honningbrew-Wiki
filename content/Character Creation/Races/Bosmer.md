*“To a civilized imperial, the Green Pact seems a grotesque horror. But when you look upon the towering canopies of Valenwood, you realize the forest does not belong to us. We belong to it. And the forest demands a heavy price for its protection.”*
— *Elenwin, Silvenar Chronicler*

In the vast, untamed wilderness of Valenwood, the Bosmer have rejected the rigid, cloistered civilization of their High Elf cousins in favor of a harmonious, primal existence. Known across Tamriel as peerless scouts, ultimate survivalists, and unmatched marksmen, the Wood Elves possess a playful, nimble wit that belies a fiercely deadly nature. They are uniquely defined by their absolute devotion to the ancient deity Y'ffre, the Storyteller and God of the Forest. Through a sacred, ancient covenant, the Bosmer have bound their society to the literal lifeblood of their homeland, adopting a lifestyle that outsiders find both profoundly spiritual and deeply unsettling.

> [!info] The Green Pact and the Meat Mandate
> The foundational pillar of Bosmer society is the Green Pact, a strict treaty signed with Y'ffre in exchange for the forest's shifting favor. Under the Pact, Bosmer are strictly forbidden from harming, harvesting, or consuming any plant life within the borders of Valenwood; to chop down a tree or clear a bush is a capital religious crime. To survive, the Bosmer are strict, absolute carnivores. Furthermore, the Pact dictates the "Meat Mandate"—no part of a fallen foe or hunted prey may ever go to waste. Bosmer fashion their clothing, armor, and structural beams out of bone, hide, and sinew, and they are religiously bound to completely consume the flesh of those they slay in battle within three days. To refuse this mandate is to risk breaking the treaty and undoing the stable forms of their entire people.

### Masters of the Canopy
Because of their absolute reliance on hunting, the Bosmer have evolved a supernatural affinity for navigation and archery, with some historical scholars even crediting them as the original creators of the bow. They treat the massive, continent-spanning trees of Valenwood as highway systems, moving through the upper branches with a soundless grace that catches even the most vigilant intruders off guard. They are a communal, generally peaceful people when left alone, preferring small, interconnected tree-settlements over sprawling stone empires, and they frequently keep the local forest fauna as beloved companions.

### Names & Culture
Unlike other Elven races who carry heavy lineage names, Bosmer names are simple, consisting of a first name only. They view their actions and deeds as their true legacy rather than an inherited family title.

### Bosmer Traits
As a Bosmer, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Standing between 5’0" and 5’8" feet tall, Bosmer are among the most compact races in Tamriel, born with skin tones ranging from rich earthy browns and pale tans to subtle shades of light mossy green. Some individuals even sprout small, stag-like horns from their brows.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Bosmeris.
