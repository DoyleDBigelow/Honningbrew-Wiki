*“You think you know hardship, outlander? You have not watched your skies turn to choking ash. You have not buried your ancestors beneath a bleeding mountain. Do not expect our hospitality when your kind has done nothing but look upon our sorrow with greed.”*
— *Brelyna Maryon, House Telvanni*

Hailing from the harsh, now volcanic wasteland, of Morrowind, the Dunmer—or Dark Elves—are a grim, fiercely proud, and intensely aloof people. Known for their striking ash-grey skin and smoldering red eyes, the Dunmer possess a combined cultural identity of aristocratic refinement and brutal survivalism. They are a deeply insular race, traditionally viewing all non-Dunmer with open disdain and deep-seated suspicion. Possessing a natural affinity for both blade and destructive magic, the Dark Elves have weathered divine curses, endless foreign invasions, and catastrophic environmental ruin, proving time and again that their spirits are as unyielding as the volcanic rock of their homeland.

> [!info] The Curse of Azura and the Living Gods
> In the ancient past, the Dunmer were the golden-skinned **Chimer**. Led by the prophet Veloth, they left the Summerset Isles to worship the "Good Daedra"—Azura, Mephala, and Boethiah. However, their society shifted drastically when three mortal sorcerers—Vivec, Almalexia, and Sotha Sil—stole divine power from the Heart of Lorkhan to set themselves up as the living gods of the Tribunal. Infuriated by this betrayal of the ancient compacts, the Daedric Prince Azura leveled a devastating cosmic curse upon the entire race. In an instant, their golden skin was turned to the color of ash, and their eyes burned red like fire—a perpetual reminder of their hubris. In the modern era, following the fall of the Tribunal, the Dunmer have returned to their ancient roots, renaming their original Daedric patrons **The Reclamations**.

### A Shattered Empire
For centuries, Dunmer society was defined by a rigid, powerful Great House system and the widespread, institutionalized slavery of beast-folk, particularly Argonians. However, the recent era has brought unprecedented tragedy to Morrowind. The catastrophic eruption of **Red Mountain** (known as the Red Year) completely decimated the province's landscape, burying ancestral cities in ash. Seizing the moment of chaos, the liberated Argonians of Black Marsh launched a massive, vengeful invasion from the south. Today, the Dunmer find themselves a displaced people; many have fled to neighboring provinces like Skyrim, where they are forced to endure bitter segregation, cold hostility, and rampant xenophobia in cities like Windhelm.

### Names & Culture
Dunmer culture places immense spiritual value on ancestral lineage and house loyalty. They always carry a given first name and a prominent family surname tied to their bloodline or Great House affiliation.

### Dunmer Traits
As a Dunmer, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Dunmer are a slightly more compact elven race, typically standing between 5'5" and 5'9" feet tall. They are instantly recognizable by their striking ashen-gray to charcoal skin and sharp, angular facial features framed by deep, sunken eyes that glow with shades of crimson and red.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Tamrielic (Common) and Dunmeris.
