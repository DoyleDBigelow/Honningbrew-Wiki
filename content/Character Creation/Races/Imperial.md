Hailing from the fertile, cosmopolitan heartland of Cyrodiil, the Imperials are the driving political and bureaucratic force of Tamriel. As the architects of the Septim and Mede Empires, they have wielded vast authority across the continent for eras. Imperials are a remarkably educated, disciplined, and diplomatic populace who hold a profound respect for law, civic order, and structured society. While they may lack the raw, primal physical power of the Nords or the innate martial artistry of the Redguards, they are a phenomenal warrior race whose true strength lies in the collective. They are unmatched generals, peerless tacticians, and shrewd diplomats who conquer threats not through reckless individual heroism, but through flawless planning, discipline, and strategic cunning.

> [!info] The Alessian Slave Rebellion
> The grand civilization of Cyrodiil was forged in the fires of absolute tyranny. Centuries ago, the human ancestors of the Imperials were brutalized slaves, held under the cruel, magical yoke of the **Ayleids**—the ancient, [[Daedra|Daedra]]-worshipping Wild Elves who ruled the heartland. Led by the slave-queen Alessia and championed by the divine crusader Pelinal Whitestrake, the human populations rose in a monumental rebellion. They overthrew their elven masters, shattered the Ayleid city-states, and seized the White-Gold Tower. From the ashes of their enslavement, they established the first human empire, permanently shifting the balance of power across Tamriel.

### The Shield of the Legion
Imperial culture is heavily defined by its civic duty and military organization. The Imperial Legion is the gold standard of military efficiency, utilizing heavily armored heavy infantry formations that rely on absolute teamwork and mutual support. This structured lifestyle extends into their civilian world, where trade, complex legal codes, and high education flourish. Imperials are masterful merchants and silver-tongued diplomats, preferring to bring independent provinces into the fold through treaties, trade monopolies, and political marriages before resorting to the devastating march of their legions.

### Names & Culture
Imperial names are heavily rooted in ancient history, utilizing distinct Latin and classical European naming conventions. They always utilize both a given first name and a traditional, grand family surname.

### Imperial Traits
As an Imperial, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Imperials possess the physical build of average humans, typically standing between 5’7" and 5’11" feet tall. However, they carry themselves with a highly disciplined, dignified bearing and place a strong cultural emphasis on immaculate hygiene, tailored clothing, and polished armor.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Tamrielic (Common) and one extra language of your choice.
