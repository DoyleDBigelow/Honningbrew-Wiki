# Nords

*"A Nord measures the truth by the bite of his axe, the warmth of his hearth, and the fire in his belly. We have broken dragons, outlasted the frozen wastes, and we will still be here when your stone empires crumble to dust.”*
— *Torvald, Storm-Bear of Windhelm*

Hailing from the frozen, jagged peaks and unforgiving tundras of Skyrim, the Nords are a fierce, hearty human race renowned across Tamriel for their unmatched physical endurance, martial pride, and stoic resilience. Born into a land where winter is eternal and survival is a daily battle, Nords are a deeply traditional and spiritual people who view life through the lens of individual honor, ancestral glory, and the promise of a warrior's afterlife. To outsiders, they can appear boisterous, headstrong, and intensely suspicious of outsiders—particularly elven magic-users. Yet, beneath their gruff, weather-beaten exteriors lies a deeply passionate people who celebrate life with thunderous song, roaring fires, and an unbreakable loyalty to their clan and country.

> [!info] The Dragon War and the Gift of the Thu'um
> In the ancient Merethic Era, the human ancestors of the Nords were brutally enslaved by the Dragon Cult—a tyrannical hierarchy of priests who ruled in absolute devotion to the dragons themselves. Facing total annihilation, the goddess **Kynareth** (the Nord aspect of Kyne) took pity on mankind. She commanded the dragon Paarthurnax to teach the early Nords how to manipulate the vocal tonal architecture of the universe—the **Thu'um**, or the Storm-Voice. Channeling their inner life essence into devastating draconic shouts, the legendary heroes of Skyrim waged a bloody war of liberation, casting down the dragon priests and overthrowing their immortal masters in a monumental victory that cemented the Nords as the ultimate dragon-slayers of history.

### The Return of the Atmorans
The Nords do not claim Tamriel as their original birthplace. They trace their ancestry back to the mythical, frozen continent of Atmora far to the north. Driven out by an encroaching, apocalyptic ice age, the legendary King Ysgramor led the historic "Five Hundred Companions" across the Sea of Ghosts to reclaim Skyrim. After wiping out the hostile Snow Elf civilizations that threatened their early settlements, the Nords forged a massive, enduring warrior culture that eventually formed the bedrock of the first human empire on Tamriel. They honor this legacy through a profound respect for martial prowess, physical strength, and the meticulous craftsmanship of heavy iron and steel weaponry.

### Names & Culture
True Nords rarely utilize traditional, inherited family surnames. Instead, their identity is bound to their personal deeds, utilizing vivid titles, physical descriptions, or clan affiliations that are added either before or after their given name (frequently hyphenated as suffixes, such as *Bjorn Stone-Fist* or *Ysolda the Fair*). These titles can shift and evolve over a Nord's lifetime as they achieve new feats of valor.

### Nord Traits
As a Nord, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Nords are among the largest and most physically imposing of the human races, typically standing between 5’8" and 6’3" feet tall. They possess heavily muscled frames, remarkably pale skin adapted to the northern light, and thick, braided hair ranging from flaxen blonde to deep chestnut.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Tamrielic (Common).
