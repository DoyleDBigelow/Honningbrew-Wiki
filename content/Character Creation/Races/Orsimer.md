*“Let the weak hide behind their high stone walls and speak of treaties. An Orc relies on the strength of his clan, the weight of his hammer, and the cold iron in his blood. We have been cast out, hunted, and broken a thousand times—yet we still stand.”*
— *Chief Yamarz, Inversed Stronghold*

Known to themselves as the *Orismer*, Orcs are a proud, fiercely independent people whose history is written in blood, exile, and unparalleled martial resilience. Though outsiders long discarded them as mere brutish monsters or simple beast-folk, Orcs are fundamentally elves, possessing a tragic, ancient heritage that shapes their stoic outlook on Tamriel. Unmatched in frontline warfare and legendary for their masterful blacksmithing, Orcs live by a rigid code of honor and self-reliance. They are the ultimate survivors, turning the isolated, jagged mountain peaks of Tamriel into fortresses where the weak do not last and the strong endure.

> [!info] The Fall of Trinimac and the Pariah God
> Long ago, the Orismer were proud Aldmer who followed **Trinimac**, the greatest warrior champion of the elven pantheon. In a cataclysmic clash of divine ideals, the [[Daedra|Daedric Prince]] Boethiah defeated Trinimac, fundamentally corrupting his spirit and body. When Trinimac emerged from [[The Shadow|the shadow]] of that defeat, he was transformed into **Malacath**, the [[Daedra|Daedric Prince]] of the Spurned and the Ostracized. At that exact moment, his faithful followers were physically twisted alongside him, their fair elven features warping into green skin and prominent tusks. Since that day, the Orcs have been the ultimate pariahs of Tamriel, a people defined by the bitter, protective fury of their outcast god.

### Strongholds and the Endless Siege
Because of their fearsome reputation, the history of the Orismer is a cycle of rebuilding and ruin. Whenever Orcs unite to build a proper civilization—most notably the legendary city-state of Orsinium—neighboring human and elven provinces launch preemptive, genocidal military raids to crush them before they can become a massive military threat. Consequently, most Orcs live in isolated mountain strongholds spread throughout Skyrim, High Rock, and Hammerfell. These small societies are fiercely traditional, strictly clan-based, and ruled by a rigid hierarchy where a chieftain must constantly prove his strength, or be replaced by a younger, stronger warrior. 

### Names & Culture
Orc names are highly individualistic, though they rarely carry traditional human family names. Instead, an Orc's given name is typically followed by a patronymic or matronymic prefix (*gro-* for "son of", *gra-* for "daughter of") or a specific clan title denoting the stronghold they were born into.

### Orc Traits
As an Orc, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Orcs possess massive, muscular frames and a distinct goblinesque appearance, characterized by thick green skin and large, prominent bottom canines or tusks. They typically stand between 5’10" and 6’2" feet tall.
* **Age.** Orcs have a lifespan similar to that of humans, reaching physical maturity in their late teens and usually living less than a century.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet. Your speed is not reduced while wearing heavy armor.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Orcish. Orcish is a harsh, grating language dominated by hard consonants.
