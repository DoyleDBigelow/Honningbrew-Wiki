# Argonians

*“You look upon the Black Marsh and see a putrid, festering swamp. We look upon it and see the emerald womb of the world. Do not think us simple because we do not build cities of stone, dry-skin; the roots of the Hist run deeper than the foundations of your entire Empire.”*
— *Uxith-Ei, Keeper of the Root*

Deep within the treacherous, toxic miasma of the Black Marsh dwells the enigmatic race of the Argonians—or the *Saxhleel*, as they call themselves in their native tongue. Possessing a cold, calculating exterior that often masks a deep, profound spirituality, the Argonians are a resilient, reptilian people uniquely adapted to survive in one of the most hostile regions of Tamriel. They are entirely distinct from both men and mer, viewing the passage of time, property, and life through a fluid, cyclical lens. While often treated with ignorance or cruelty by outsiders, the Saxhleel are unmatched guerilla warriors, exceptional survivors, and fiercely loyal protectors of their primordial homeland.

> [!info] The People of the Root
> The defining truth of Argonian existence is their symbiotic bond with the **Hist**—a sentient, ancient race of spore-bearing trees native to the heart of the Black Marsh. Argonians do not merely revere the Hist; they were created by them. As eggs, Argonians are placed into sacred birthing pools beneath the boughs of the Hist, where they consume the trees' sap. This sap shapes their physical minds, grants them a collective spiritual connection to the roots of the marsh, and determines their physiological development. To be severed from the Hist is considered by the Saxhleel to be a fate far worse than death, as the trees are the keepers of their immortal souls.

### The Shadowscales and the Tribal Life
While most Argonians live a traditional, localized tribal lifestyle governed by the seasons of the swamp, their society possesses deep, ancient traditions. Chief among these is the legacy of the **Shadowscales**. Any Argonian born under the sign of *The Shadow* is immediately separated from their tribe at birth and offered to the Dark Brotherhood. Trained from infancy in the arts of stealth, assassination, and poison, these elite killers serve as the ultimate enforcement of law and vengeance within the Black Marsh. Though a Shadowscale may legally leave the guild once they reach adulthood, they remain feared and deeply respected figures across Tamriel.

### Names & Culture
Argonian naming conventions are highly diverse and usually fall into three distinct categories depending on how much contact the individual's tribe has with the outside world:
* **Single-Word Jel Names:** Ancient, untranslatable terms that describe a core trait or cosmic alignment.
* **Hyphenated Jel Names:** Multi-part descriptions of a physical feature or action (e.g., *Xal-Tei*).
* **Tamrielic / Cyrodilic Names:** Direct translations of their native names into Common words (e.g., *Walks-In-Swamps* or *Hides-Her-Eyes*).
* **Naming Conventions:** [UESP Argonian Names Reference](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Lore:Argonian_Names)

### Argonian Traits
As an Argonian, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Argonians are reptilian in appearance, possessing thick scales, clawed digits, and expressive, lizard-like faces. Their heads are frequently adorned with protective horn-spikes, crests, or even colorful, vibrant feathers. 
* **Growth and Lifespan.** Although their ultimate lifespans are comparable to humans, young Argonians develop at an astonishingly rapid rate. They can walk within hours of hatching, attain the physical size of a 10-year-old human child by the age of 3, and reach full maturity by the age of 15.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Jel.
