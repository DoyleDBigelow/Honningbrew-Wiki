*“You measure your lives in seasons, little human. You scramble, you build wooden shacks, and you rot before the mortar settles. We do not merely build; we refine. We do not merely learn; we perfect. When you look upon Summerset, you are looking at the unbroken memory of heaven.”*
— *Ancarin, Justiciar of the Third Aldmeri Dominion*

Hailing from the pristine, sun-drenched archipelago of the Summerset Isles, the Altmer—or High Elves, as they are known to the shorter-lived races—are a proud, elegant, and exceptionally graceful people. They consider themselves the direct, unmutilated biological and spiritual descendants of the ancient Et'Ada, the divine spirits who forged Mundus. With an unbroken cultural lineage stretching back to the mythical continent of Aldmeris, the Altmer possess a supernatural aptitude for the arcane, making them the most universally powerful sorcerers on Tamriel. To outsiders, they can appear mysterious, fiercely guarded, and intensely reserved, shielding their centuries of accumulated secrets, artistic techniques, and magical philosophies from the eyes of non-Altmer.

> [!info] A Perspective of Stasis
> The core of Altmer philosophy is *Anuic Stasis*—the preservation of perfect, unyielding divine order. While other elven races are viewed by the Altmer as "degenerated" through the worship of Daedra or chaotic cultural drift, the High Elves strive to mirror the immaculate hierarchy of the heavens. Their cities are staggering monuments of glittering glass, coral, and white stone, filled with a cultural obsession for art, magic, and absolute beauty. Because they view time not as a scarce resource to be spent, but as a canvas for ultimate perfection, they do not merely practice a craft; they seek to embody it entirely. 

### The Cradled Lineage and Goblin Labor
Altmer society is a rigid, aristocratic hierarchy strictly regimented by classification. At the pinnacle are the Wise (teachers and high priests of the Aedric pantheon), followed closely by the Artists, the Princes (noble administrators), the Warriors and Merchants, and finally the manual laborers. While they look down on the overt, brutal slavery practiced by the Dunmer of Morrowind, the Altmer have a long, hypocritical history of utilizing subjugated Goblin tribes as a slave-labor force to maintain the pristine beauty of their cities, viewing Goblins as little more than clever animals suited only for toil. 

### Names & Culture
Altmer names are deeply tied to their long-standing heritage, valuing family lineage immensely. Because their true, formal names are incredibly lengthy and detail centuries of ancestry, most Altmer tend to omit or hide their family names from outsiders, displaying only a single given name in daily life.

### Altmer Traits
As an Altmer, you have the following racial traits:
* **Creature Type.** You are a Humanoid.
* **Size.** You are Medium. Altmer are the tallest of the sapient races, typically standing well over 6 feet and occasionally exceeding 7 feet. Despite their height, they possess a slender, aristocratic build (typically weighing between 140 and 180 pounds) with sharp, angular features, almond-shaped golden eyes, and skin tones ranging from pale gold to deep bronze. 
* **Age.** Altmer reach physical maturity at the same rate as humans, but are not socially considered "adults" until at least 30. They typically live 350 to 450 years, though masters of Restorative magic or those who have "calibrated" their spirits can live for millennia.
* **Alignment.** Most Altmer lean heavily toward Lawful Neutral, placing supreme value on tradition, divine order, and the preservation of lineage.
* **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
* **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Tamrielic (Common) and Altmeris.
