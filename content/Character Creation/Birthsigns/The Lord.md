Characters born under this Birthsign have the following adjustments:
- You have +1 AC.
- You have +1 to ranged/melee hit chance.
- Your unarmed strikes are D4+Str rolls, instead of D2+Str rolls.
- You are proficient in all Armors, Weapons, and Shields. Even when improvised, conjured, or broken. It's not skill to you, it's raw intuition.
- You have [[Vulnerability]] to fire.
