Characters born under this Birthsign have the following adjustments:
- *Cheater's Nip* - Once per day, for a whole 2 minutes increase your Dexterity and Charisma by 4pts (max of 24) When this effect ends roll a d6 and suffer this after effect for abusing greater powers.

| Roll (1d6) | After Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1          | The next time you take damage, it is treated like a critical hit. You may also remove this effect by becoming drunk. An ally may not hit you to remove this effect.                                                                                                              |
| 2          | The next time you long rest, your sleep is disturbed by visions of the Daedric Lord of Domination, Molag Bal. You awake with a single level of exhaustion.                                                                                                                       |
| 3          | You lose the ability to speak as the character for the next real life 5 minutes.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 4          | You feel vulnerable in the company of others. Reduce your charisma by 2 until your next long rest. This effect cannot be easily undone.                                                                                                                                          |
| 5          | The DM flips a coin behind their screen. If the flip is heads, when character makes the next ability check declare it was heads and nothing happens. If the flip is tails interrupt the next ability check made and replace its roll with an 8, then add any relevant modifiers. |
| 6          | Nothing happens.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
