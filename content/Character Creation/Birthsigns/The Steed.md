Characters born under this Birthsign have the following adjustments:
- *I am your Beast* - You can comfortably pull carts and seat 2 passengers just like a horse!
- *Uncanny Strength* - +1 Str, +10 movement speed.
- *Obvious* - You regain up to a max of 2 charges after a long rest. When an ally no further than 15ft from you, is the target of a spell, ability, or an attack at range you may expend a charge to become the target of the attack instead.
