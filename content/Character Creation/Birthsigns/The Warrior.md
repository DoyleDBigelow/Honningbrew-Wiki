Characters born under this Birthsign have the following adjustments:
- *War Cry* - At the start of combat (when you roll initiative), you may let out a mighty battle cry. If you do, your AC is decreased by 1 for the combat. However:
  - Non-magical melee or ranged attacks dealt by your allies deal +2 magic damage. This does not stack with other *War Cry* buffs.
  - When you or your allies deal non-magical melee or ranged attacks to an enemy, any wielder of *War Cry* heals 1d6+1 HP.
