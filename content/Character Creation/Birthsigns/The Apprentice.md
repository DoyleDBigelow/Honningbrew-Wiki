Characters born under this Birthsign have the following adjustments:
- You have 50% more Magicka.
- You have a [[Vulnerability]] to magic.
- Tenacity - If a spell, such as Fireball (A damage spell, with an area of effect) would deal half damage to your character you may whittle the damage down to 0 at the cost of 1 Magicka. Only works when conscious.
