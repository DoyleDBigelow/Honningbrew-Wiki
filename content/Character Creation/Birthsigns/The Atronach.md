Characters born under this Birthsign have the following adjustments:
- You have 100% more Magicka.
- You cannot generate Magicka passively.
- You have 50% Spell Absorption, meaning when your character is the receiver of magical impact half of the effect is fizzled and absorbed as Magicka. Spells generally restore Magicka based on how much Magicka they cost. 2nd Level spells cost 3 Magicka. If you were to absorb half rounded up you would restore 2 Magicka. Cantrips only restore your Magicka if they harm/heal you.
