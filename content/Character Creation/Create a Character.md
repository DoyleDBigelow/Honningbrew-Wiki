In order to make a Elder Scrolls Paper (ESP) Character, you'll need to visit these pages to slowly bring your creation to life. Each page has more documentation to help you achieve your dreams!
- Class - the job your character occupies. [[Class Handbook]] 
- Birthsign - the stars you were born under bestow upon you a specific gift. [[Birthsign Handbook]]
- All players get access to their choice of one Restoration cantrip and one Destruction cantrip as all TES characters start with some basic spells.
- Magic classes each have to select spells to use, and are expected to readily have spell knowledge available. 
- Background, this does not offer skills like traditional D&D. This offers you an item loadout to have at the start of the game. Usually tied to a motivation for your character to be adventuring.
- Select two skills for your character to be proficient in.
