# Act 1: The Kingless Peak
Players start on a prison transport boat heading to Cyrodiil through the Niben Bay.
Unbeknownst to the players there is also a powerful artifact upon the ship. The Staff of Magnus.
A clamor starts on the deck. A few moments later a cannonball blast through the wall, shattering the lock, and smashing into the guard. The players are then able to recover their items and remove their shackles.
The players have to navigate through a room to get top deck. This room has 3 guards inside guarding a illustrious gilded staff case. This serves as a tutorial combat and players should be heavily incentivised to open the chest and take the staff.  
## **The Escape**
Players flee the Thalmor with the Staff of Magnus. The Psijic Order reaches out, explaining that the "pins" are failing and the world is becoming physically unstable.

### **The Hook**
Players escape a Thalmor transport with the Staff of Magnus. The Psijic Order intervenes, explaining that the world is "slipping" and they need to gather Aedric artifacts to act as temporary anchors.

## **The Quest**
Players travel to retrieve Aedric artifacts.

## **The Catastrophe**
The world shakes with the power of thuum. Paarthanax has been killed and the snow throat sundered. This continues for a minute but then the sheer force causes the Throat of the World to crumble, the peak collapsing into a massive pile of rubble and dust. The Shouts stop instantly. Reality begins to "unravel"—laws of nature (gravity, weather, time) start to glitch locally.

## Act 2: The Forbidden Vault
## The Mission
The Psijics explain that with the mountain tower gone, they need a new power source to stabilize the world. They send the players to find the Clockwork Heart.

## The Trade
Players bargain with Hermaeus Mora, trading an Elder Scroll for the location/path to the Clockwork City.

### The Recovery
Deep within the City, the players find the Clockwork Heart along with Sunder and Keening.

# Act 3: The Siege of Adamantine (The False Climax)
### The Holdout
The Thalmor move on the Adamantine Tower. The Psijics tell the players: "We are installing the Heart. You must hold the Tower, or the world unravels before the reboot is ready."

### The Dungeon Crawl
A brutal defensive crawl. The players feel the weight of being the "last line of defense."

### The Reveal
As the Tower's structural integrity hits zero, the sky tears open. The Numidium emerges. To the players, this looks like a new threat or a miracle, until the Psijics reveal this is the "vessel" for the Heart they just fetched.

# Act 4: The 5th Era Rending
## The Final Piece
The players must secure the Totem of Tiber Septim (hidden in the chaos of the siege) to interface with the machine.

## The **Restoration**
The Numidium uses the Eye of Magnus to scan the "unraveling threads" of the world. Using the Staff and the Aedric Artifacts, the players guide the machine to "stitch" the Aedra back into the foundation of Nirn.
