The **Magna Ge** (Children of Magnus) are the [[Et'Ada]] who initially joined the creation of [[Mundus]] but fled when they realized the cost. Led by **Magnus**, the Architect, they tore through the veil of the [[Aurbis]] to return to [[Aetherius]] (the realm of pure light). The holes they left behind are the stars, and the largest hole—left by Magnus himself—is the Sun. They are the source of magic in the mortal world, as the light of [[Aetherius]] leaks through these "wounds" in reality.
