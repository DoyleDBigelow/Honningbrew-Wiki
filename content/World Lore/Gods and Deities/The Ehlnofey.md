# The Lesser Aedra
The **Ehlnofey** (Ehlnofex for "Earth Bones") are the collective of lesser [[Aedra|Aedric Spirits]] who remained on [[Mundus]] after the [[Magne Ge]] fled and the Eight [[Divines]] established the Spokes of [[Aurbis|The Wheel]]. While the [[Divines]] maintained their celestial individuality, the Ehlnofey stayed to inhabit the world, becoming the "substance" of the mortal plane.

## The Aedric Superset
In the literalist cosmology, the Ehlnofey represent the **Aedric Residue**. They are the spirits who were too involved in the "project" to leave, but not powerful enough to remain as infinite celestial bodies. Over time, this superset fractured into two distinct paths:

### 1. The Earth Bones (The Foundation)
Many Ehlnofey followed the sacrifice of **Y'ffre**. They surrendered their sentience and names to become the immutable laws of the world. Without this sacrifice, the world would have remained a shifting "Gray Maybe" where gravity, time, and biology had no consistency.
* *See [[Earth Bones]] for more details.*

### 2. The Ancestors (The Mortal Subset)
The remaining Ehlnofey retained their consciousness and names but began to "diminish" in scale to survive within the finite limits of [[Mundus]]. This group further split during the **Ehlnofey War**:
* **Old Ehlnofey:** Those who stayed in the ancestral lands (Aldmeris), striving to maintain their divine purity. They are the progenitors of the **Mer** (Elves).
* **Wandering Ehlnofey:** Those who were scattered across the face of Nirn, adapting and changing to survive the harsh wilderness. They are the progenitors of **Men** (Humans).
