The **Earth Bones** are a specific subset of the [[The Ehlnofey]] who sacrificed their individual identity to stabilize the chaotic overlap of the Dawn Era. They are the "Sacrificed Aedra" who chose to become the literal functions of the universe rather than watch the [[Mundus]] collapse.

## The First Bone: Y'ffre
The spirit **Y'ffre** (the Storyteller) was the first to "transform" into a Bone. Before Y'ffre, the world was formless; spirits changed shape constantly, and the "Green" had no set pattern. By becoming the first Earth Bone, Y'ffre established the **Green Pact** of nature—giving trees their shape and giving the world its "story" (sequence and logic).

## Functional Nature
Unlike the [[Divines]], you cannot speak to an Earth Bone. They do not have avatars or personalities. Instead, they are expressed through:
* **The Laws of Physics:** Gravity, thermodynamics, and the passage of time on the surface of [[Nirn]].
* **The Biological Blueprint:** The reason a seed grows into a specific plant or why a creature's heart beats in a specific rhythm.
* **Tonal Constants:** The fundamental frequencies that Tonal Architects (like the Dwemer) or Tongues (like the Nords) manipulate to alter reality.

## The Difference Between Earth Bones and Mortals
While both are descended from the [[The Ehlnofey]], the distinction lies in the **Finality of Sacrifice**:
1. **The Earth Bone** is a spirit that gave up its *Mind* to become *Matter*.
2. **The Mortal** is a spirit that gave up its *Power* to keep its *Mind*.
