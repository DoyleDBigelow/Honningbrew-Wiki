# The Primordial Duality
Before the beginning, there were the Two. They are not "gods" with personalities, but the ultimate dualistic forces of the [[Aurbis]]—the friction between them is what allows the "Maybe" to exist.

## Anu (The Everything)
Anu is the **"I AM"**—the primordial fullness, stasis, and unchanging order. While often simplified as "Light," Anu more accurately represents the **Pleroma**: the infinite point where every possibility exists simultaneously in a state of perfect, unmoving unity. In deep Altmeri metaphysics, Anu is the "Everything" that existed before the first "No." He is the source of all substance; without his infinite "Is," the Void of Padomay would be empty of potential. Anu is the stone from which all reality is carved.

## Padomay (The Void)
Padomay is the **"I AM NOT"**—the force of change, darkness, and chaotic subversion. While often used interchangeably with **Sithis**, Padomay represents the "Void" that predates identity. He is the force that prevents Anu from being a stagnant, singular point of nothingness. In the deep lore of the Dark Brotherhood or the Psijic Order, this force is the **"Start of All Things,"** for without change, there can be no sequence, no time, and no beginning. Padomay is the knife that carves the stone of Anu.

## The Creation of Reality: Aurbis
The eternal interplay and struggle between these two (often personified as the "clash of the brothers") resulted in the creation of the **[[Aurbis]]**. 

Because "Everything" (Anu) and "Nothing" (Padomay) cannot directly interact without annihilating one another, they produced sub-gradients to act as their souls and mediators:
* **Anui-El:** The soul of Anu, representing the will to stabilize.
* **Sithis:** The soul of Padomay, representing the will to diversify.

From the interplay of these two souls arose the **[[Et'Ada]]** (Original Spirits). These spirits then began the long process of stabilization that would eventually lead to the creation of [[Aurbis|The Wheel]], the Towers, and the [[Mundus|Mortal World]].
