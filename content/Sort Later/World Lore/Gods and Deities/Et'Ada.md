The Et'Ada, or "Original Spirits," are the primordial beings that emerged from the interplay of the twin cosmic forces: [[Anu and Padomay|Anu]] (Stasis/Light) and [[Anu and Padomay|Padomay]] (Change/Dark). Before the existence of time, space, or the mortal plane, these spirits crystallized within the [[Aurbis]] (the "Gray Maybe"). They vary wildly in power and nature, representing fundamental concepts of the universe. While the earliest and most powerful spirits—such as Akatosh and Lorkhan — defined the initial structure of reality, the [[Et'Ada]] eventually fractured into distinct groups based on their involvement in the creation of the mortal world, [[Mundus]].

The Great Schism of the [[Et'Ada]] occurred when Lorkhan convinced, or tricked, many of the spirits into sacrificing their divinity to create a permanent, stable realm. Those who gave of themselves to become the bones of the earth and the ancestors of the mortal races are known as the [[Aedra]] (meaning "Our Ancestors" in Ehlnofex). Conversely, those who refused to participate, retaining their full power and autonomy within their own pocket-dimensions of [[Oblivion]], are known as the [[Daedra]] ("Not Our Ancestors"). A third group, the [[Magna Ge]], followed the architect Magnus as he fled the project, tearing holes through the sky to [[Aetherius]]—the holes mortals perceive as stars. This fundamental division defines the metaphysical tension that drives all conflict in Elder Scrolls cosmology.
