The **Daedra** ("Not Our Ancestors") are those spirits who refused to participate in the creation of [[Mundus]]. They retained their full power and sovereignty, residing within the void of [[Oblivion]]. Because they gave nothing of themselves to the creation of the mortal world, they cannot "create" in the same sense as the [[Aedra]]; they can only alter, mimic, or subvert existing forms. They are often associated with chaos and the ever changing of the [[Mundus|Mortal World]].
