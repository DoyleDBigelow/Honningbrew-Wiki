In the deepest circles of Altmeri thought, this structure is viewed as a cage of limitation. They believe that before the "Wheel" was forced upon them, they were infinite spirits. To them, the [[Mundus|Mundus]] is a stabilization that led to their "fall" into mortality.

**Their goal is to reverse the process of creation:**
- **Unmake the Towers:** By deactivating the Towers that pin the Hub in place, they intend to collapse the structural integrity of [[Mundus|Mundus]].
- **Dissolve the Wheel:** Without the Hub, the "Spokes" (Aedra) lose their purpose and the "Spaces" (Oblivion) bleed back into the whole.
- **Return to Divinity:** With the Wheel unmade, the [[Aurbis|Aurbis]] returns to its original state of chaotic potential. In this formless "Maybe," the Elves believe they will shed their mortal "mutilation" and exist once more as eternal gods, no longer bound by the laws of a structured universe.
